Our programs

Four areas of research and transmission

01

African Antiquity Program

Research on:

  • The earliest African civilizations
  • African kingdoms and empires
  • Ancient African sciences
  • Traditional political and social systems
02

African Languages Program

  • Promotion of Mali's national languages
  • Linguistic documentation
  • Training in N'Ko, Adlam, Tifinagh, Ge'ez, Vai and Bamum scripts
  • Production of educational content
03

Culture & Heritage Program

  • Heritage conservation
  • Digital archives
  • Cultural conferences
  • Scientific publications
04

Youth & Innovation Program

  • Training
  • Cultural competitions
  • Entrepreneurial initiatives
  • Cultural leadership
