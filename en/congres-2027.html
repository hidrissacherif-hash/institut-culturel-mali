A landmark gathering for African research

International Congress on African Antiquity

"The influence of ancient Africa on the world through its languages, culture and science"
Bamako, Mali · November 16 – 20, 2027
Objectives

Why this congress

  • Raise awareness of Africa's ancient civilizations
  • Study their scientific and cultural contributions
  • Promote languages and systems of knowledge transmission
  • Foster research on African heritage
  • Build collaboration between African and international researchers
  • Adopt the Bamako Declaration on African Antiquity
Program

Five days in Bamako

Nov 16 — Day 1
Opening ceremony and plenary sessions
Nov 17 — Day 2
Academic sessions: ancient civilizations and languages
Nov 18 — Day 3
Academic sessions: sciences, governance and heritage
Nov 19 – 20
Academic and cultural field program
Field program

Three sites, three traditions of knowledge

Site 1

Dogon Country

Architecture, cosmology, traditional knowledge and astronomical understanding.

Site 2

Kéla

Center of oral tradition, preservation and transmission of the history of the Mali Empire.

Site 3

Kouroukan Fouga

Foundation of the Manden Charter, governance, law and community responsibility.
