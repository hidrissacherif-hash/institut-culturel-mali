Governance

Board of Directors

The Board of Directors is the collegial body responsible for the governance, strategic direction, oversight and control of the Institut Culturel du Mali – Lonniya Guèndè. It sets the Institute's overall direction, ensures its programs remain aligned with its mission, and oversees the General Management in accordance with ICM's Statutes and internal regulations.

Composed of eleven members chosen for their professional expertise, institutional experience and knowledge of Africa and its heritage, the Board meets at least once a year and may form specialized committees covering finance, programs, partnerships and governance.

Mamadou Cherif Haidara

Member of the Board of Directors

A native of Bamako, Mamadou Cherif Haidara is a trained jurist and a retired senior banking executive with nearly thirty years in the sector. His career took him to represent banking institutions in Central Africa and in France, giving him deep international experience of the continent's financial and institutional affairs.

Alongside his banking career, he held diplomatic functions in Central Africa and was active in Malian sporting life as a director of Stade Malien, one of the country's historic football clubs. An accomplished footballer in his youth, he played for Mali's national team before pursuing a professional career in France.

Recognized for his philanthropic engagement, Mamadou Cherif Haidara now brings his experience and network to the Institut Culturel du Mali. A passionate advocate of agriculture, travel and history, he brings to the Board a perspective rooted in Malian tradition and open to international dynamics.
